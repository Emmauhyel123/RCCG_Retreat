What the retreat covers

Built around one question: what does it take to be a vessel God can use?

  • Spiritual renewal & consecration
  • Music training & capacity building
  • Vocal training
  • Music rudiments, theory & ear training
  • Instrumental training
  • Choir development & teamwork
  • Worship leadership & ministry
  • Unity, fellowship & team bonding
  • Practical musicianship & ministration
